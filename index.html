AFTEREFFECT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game AFTEREFFECT (package com.aftereffect.game) uses. The game is made by SMASH_OG ("we", "us"). Contact: open an issue at github.com/mdsarowarofficial/aftereffect-privacy/issues.

1. Short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some device information to show and measure ads (see section 3).

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (solved levels, best turns), settings (sound, music, haptics, motion), your hint count and a few counters that decide when an ad may be shown. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it. If you uninstall the game, it is deleted with the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

AFTEREFFECT uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show two kinds of ads: a short video you can choose to watch to get a hint (rewarded ad), and an ad that can appear after you finish a level (interstitial ad).

When an ad is requested, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, app version, and information about how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where allowed, personalise ads.

We ask for ads suitable for a general audience (content rating G).

You can control ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads) by resetting or deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ad personalisation. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you may be shown a consent message before personalised ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools in the game.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases at this time.

5. Children

AFTEREFFECT is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing

The only third party that receives data through the game is Google (AdMob), as described above. We do not share data with anyone else.

7. Security and retention

Because your progress stays on your device, we hold no personal data about you. Data handled by Google is governed by Google's own policies and retention rules.

8. Your choices

  • Turn off or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Uninstall the game to remove all locally saved data.
  • Contact us with any privacy question or request.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
